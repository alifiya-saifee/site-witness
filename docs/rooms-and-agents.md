## Rules every room follows

- Agents draft and prepare; **a named person decides**. Money, signatures, filings, public posts and devices that heat or move always need a person.
- Every step is an event in one log. Facts get new versions; nothing is edited or deleted.
- An approval is pinned to the facts it relied on. If one changes, the approval cancels itself and Compliance hears first.
- Site Witness never says “pass”, “safe” or “compliant”. Only a signed human verdict can.

## Today

### Front Desk

Owner assistant and WhatsApp front desk.

**Starts when:** A new WhatsApp, email or voice message · 08:00 every day

| Agent | Does | Tools | A person decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receptionist | Reads every message in English or Arabic, works out what it is about and sends it to the right room. | WhatsApp, Email, Speech to text | A reply that mentions money, dates or legal terms waits in Approvals. |
| Morning Brief | Writes the owner’s 20-second briefing from today’s facts, in English and Arabic. | Event log, Text to speech | Nothing: it only reads and reports. |

**Hands to:** Buildings, Residents, Approvals, Leads

**Never:** Promise a price, a date or a legal outcome; Reply to a client about money without approval.

### Approvals

Everything waiting for a person to decide.

**Starts when:** Any agent asks a person to decide

| Agent | Does | Tools | A person decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| Approval Clerk | Collects everything waiting for a person, shows the exact facts each item rests on, and cancels the item by itself if one of those facts changes. | Event log, Fact versions | Never decides. The named role holder taps yes or no. |

**Hands to:** Finance, Reports & Export, Deals

**Never:** Approve anything itself; Keep an approval alive after a fact it rests on has changed.

### Meetings

Recorded with consent; what was said becomes facts.

**Starts when:** A meeting starts (after everyone agrees to the recording)

| Agent | Does | Tools | A person decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scribe | Asks for consent, transcribes, and keeps each fact with the speaker’s exact words. | Speech to text (EN/AR), Event log | The people in the meeting confirm each fact. |
| Action Taker | Turns what was decided into tasks with an owner and a date. | Tasks | The meeting owner confirms the task list. |

**Hands to:** Deals, Fix jobs, Approvals

**Never:** Record without consent; Change what someone said.

**Starts when:** A walk video syncs from the engineer’s phone (it works offline)
