Devices

Scan my Wi-Fi (this Mac only)

Looks for devices on this Wi-Fi for about 6 seconds. It only listens: nothing is controlled until you add it here, with a name, a room and who may use it.

Bluetooth reaches about 10 m from the Mac (and about 10 m around each relay below). It only listens: nothing is connected until you add it. macOS asks once to let Terminal use Bluetooth.

Bluetooth relays (this Mac only)

A relay is a small ESP32 board (about AED 30–40) flashed from esphome.io as a “Bluetooth Proxy”. Put one in a far room: it hears Bluetooth there and passes it to this Mac over Wi-Fi. “Scan my Wi-Fi” finds relays too.

The key is kept only on this Mac and never shown again.

Websites (this Mac only)

The only websites the agent will open (“open Cosmique website on my Mac”). https only. You can also add one by voice: ask for it, then say its address and tap Approve.

Shared folders: your Records (this Mac only)

The folders the agent may open, search inside and send from (“open the report about the cracked balcony”, “send the lift note to Omar”). Each folder has its own list of who may use it. Nothing outside them is read. PDF, Word, text and Markdown files.

may open files from it

People (this Mac only)

Each phone is tied to one of these people when you allow it. What a person may do depends on their role.

Joined phones (this Mac only)

A right code only asks to join: nothing works until you tap Allow here. 5 wrong codes lock a device out for a minute. Phones stay joined for 30 days.

Add a robot (this Mac only)

Roborock vacuum. Type the email you use in the Roborock app; Roborock emails you a code. This logs in to Roborock’s cloud; only the login token is kept on this Mac (never your password).

ROS 2 robot (with rosbridge running). Spots on its map, one per line: Name: x, y, heading.

A lamp with a remote (this Mac only)

For things that only have an infrared remote. A Wi-Fi IR blaster (Broadlink RM4 mini) copies the remote’s buttons. Plug it in, set it up in the Broadlink app, then type its IP address here. Next, teach each button under Devices: tap “Teach”, point the remote at the blaster, and press that button once.

Spoken replies

My voice (this Mac only)

Record 5 seconds of your own voice. With NVIDIA magpie switched on, English replies are spoken in your voice. Arabic always uses the Mac’s voice. When magpie is on, your sample and the reply text are sent to NVIDIA.
